About Us

Welcome to Marietta Dental Professionals

A modern dental home for Marietta, East Cobb, Kennesaw, Woodstock, and the broader Cobb County community.

At Marietta Dental Professionals, we believe that world-class dental care should fit smoothly into your life, not complicate it. Located in Marietta, Georgia, our practice was founded with a clear purpose: to bring high quality, efficient, and compassionate dental services to individuals and families throughout Marietta, East Cobb, Kennesaw, Woodstock, and the broader Cobb County community. We know that visiting the dentist can sometimes feel overwhelming or time-consuming. That is why we have engineered a modern patient experience focused on efficiency, open communication, gentle care, and outstanding clinical results.

A Modern Approach to Dental Wellness

Dentistry is constantly evolving, and so are we. Our office is equipped with advanced dental technology, including digital imaging, intraoral cameras, and modern diagnostic tools that allow us to spot issues early and treat them with incredible precision. Digital technology allows us to show you exactly what we see inside your mouth, turning every appointment into an informative collaboration. Instead of telling you what treatment you need, we guide you through digital images and explain your options clearly, giving you full control over your health decisions.

This commitment to modern innovation extends directly to how we structure your care. We know how busy life gets, which is why we offer same-day dentistry options. Whenever feasible, we aim to complete your evaluation and treatment during the exact same appointment, saving you unnecessary extra trips and time away from work or family.

Comprehensive Care for Every Smile

Every smile is unique, and your dental care plan should reflect your specific goals and health requirements. Marietta Dental Professionals offers a full suite of dental services under one roof. Our preventive care programs, including regular exams, cleanings, and digital X-rays, lay the foundation for a lifetime of healthy habits. For patients needing tooth restoration, we offer durable fillings, custom crowns, and state-of-the-art dental implants that restore both functionality and natural aesthetics.

If you are looking to enhance your smile, our cosmetic offerings include professional teeth whitening, clear aligner solutions, and aesthetic restorations designed to boost your confidence. Furthermore, our experienced clinical team includes specialists trained in oral surgery and emergency dental care. If you experience unexpected tooth pain or an injury, our team prioritizes urgent appointments so you can receive immediate, effective relief when you need it most.

Patient Comfort and Complete Transparency

Your physical comfort and emotional peace of mind are always our top priorities. If you feel nervous about visiting the dentist, you are not alone. Our entire team is trained in gentle techniques, and we offer sedation options to make your treatments completely stress-free. We take the time to answer your questions, address your concerns, and proceed at a pace that feels comfortable for you.

We also believe that top tier dental care should be accessible and straightforward. We work with most major PPO dental insurance providers and help you navigate your coverage so you maximize your benefits. For patients without dental insurance, we offer our affordable Blueprint Smiles Membership Plan alongside flexible monthly financing choices through CareCredit, Sunbit, and Cherry. Before any procedure begins, we explain all expected costs upfront, eliminating surprise fees and helping you make financial decisions with complete clarity.

Dedicated to Serving Our Community

Marietta Dental Professionals is more than just a dental clinic. We are proud members of the local Marietta community, dedicated to fostering health, confidence, and vibrant smiles for our neighbors. Every member of our team brings genuine warmth, professional expertise, and a patient-first attitude to work each day.

Whether you are looking for a new dental home for your entire family, seeking a cosmetic transformation, or needing fast relief from a sudden toothache, we are here to serve you. Experience dental care that respects your time, values your comfort, and prioritizes your long term health. We look forward to welcoming you to Marietta Dental Professionals and giving you a reason to smile with confidence.
